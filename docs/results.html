Read this before the numbers

9 jurisdictions have at least one dimension scored as a flat 0 because that dimension's data was unavailable, not because the state measurably failed it. This is a known, unresolved issue in how the composite is assembled (see Review Dossier, Question 01). For those states in particular, treat the composite as a lower bound, not a precise score.

Only 3 of 51 jurisdictions reach the "Strong" tier under the rebuilt scoring. That's a real result of stricter, source-verified data replacing inflated or fabricated Phase 1 numbers, not a sign the rebuild is too harsh. See the Review Dossier for what changed per dimension.

What academic reviewers should do here

Expand a sample of states, particularly ones you know well or that sit near a tier boundary, and check whether the Phase 2 evidence shown actually supports the score. Pay close attention to any dimension marked Not available or Partial: those scores rest on less than full coverage. Weigh what you find against the Review Dossier's Methodology & Evidence section and Questions for Reviewers, where the open judgment calls behind this data are laid out directly.
